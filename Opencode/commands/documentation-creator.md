# Command to help me create Documentation

## Instructions

Look at coding-guidelines.md under docs/architecture. Understand in depth how it is laid out.

Now, create similar documentation. 
Ask me the title of the document and where do I want to put it. A
Ask me general criteria of this document.
I will provide you the list of items to include
Be concise but do not miss important details of each item
Ensure to maintain good layout and nice separation so its easy to follow
Ask me for the details of the list I am talking about

## Final Step

At the end, review the document thoroughly and ask me if anything does not make sense. Do this iteratively until its clear to you. If I provide corrections, update the document accordingly.
